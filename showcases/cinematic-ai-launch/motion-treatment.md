# Motion Treatment

## Intent

This chapter must feel like a product launch short, not a slide deck. Every step performs a visible transformation. Text is used as labeling and emphasis, not as the only thing happening on screen.

## Step Treatment

## Motion Rules

- Every scene needs a moving object that explains the beat.
- At least one visual object must carry continuity between adjacent steps.
- Use CSS/SVG first. Use Canvas only if the text-fragment scatter looks too mechanical.
- Keep the stage dark, spacious, and editorial. Do not add colorful UI cards.
- Motion should land before the viewer needs to read the final text.
