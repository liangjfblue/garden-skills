# Motion Treatment

## Intent

This chapter must feel like a product launch short, not a slide deck. It now tests a complex-page explanation pattern: two persistent production pages stay on screen for multiple steps. Each step first highlights the relevant card in place, then after a short hold a medium-sized focus card lifts from that source direction into the center. Text is used as labeling and emphasis, not as the only thing happening on screen.

## Motion Rules

- The chapter uses two persistent workbench pages: steps 1-4 for article-to-beats, steps 5-8 for beats-to-video.
- Each step first highlights the relevant card in place, then lifts a medium-sized center focus card after a short hold.
- Every step needs a moving object that explains the beat.
- At least one visual object must carry continuity between adjacent steps.
- Use CSS/SVG first. Use Canvas only if the text-fragment scatter looks too mechanical.
- Keep the stage dark, spacious, and editorial. Do not add colorful UI cards.
- Motion should land before the viewer needs to read the final text.
